Common Questions

How does the payment work?

I build your complete website first and show you a preview. If you like it, you pay $1,000 via secure Stripe checkout. The payment is processed through Stripe — one of the most trusted payment platforms in the world. Once payment is confirmed, your full website is unlocked and ready to go live. You see the finished product before you pay anything.

Are there any monthly fees?

No. The $1,000 is a one-time payment. You own the website outright — no subscriptions, no recurring charges, no contracts. If you later want Google Ads management, that's a separate optional service with its own pricing.

How long does it take to build my website?

Typically 48 hours from our initial consultation call. During the call, I'll gather your business info, services, photos, and preferences. Then I build the site and deliver it for your review.

What if I don't like the design?

You review the finished website before paying. If you're not satisfied, you don't pay — no obligation. I'll make revisions based on your feedback. My goal is that you're 100% happy with the result.

Do I own the website?

Yes, absolutely. Once you pay, the website is yours. You can host it anywhere, make changes yourself, or have anyone manage it. There's no lock-in or proprietary platform.

What's included in the $1,000?

Custom design with your branding, mobile-responsive layout, click-to-call functionality, contact form, SEO optimization, fast loading, and all the pages your business needs (home, about, services, contact). Everything is included — no upsells or hidden costs.

Can you help with Google Ads too?

Yes. I offer Google Ads management as a separate service with three plans: Flat-Fee Retainer ($2,000/mo), Pay-Per-Lead ($75/lead), and Revenue Share (10-20%). This is optional — many clients start with just the website and add Ads later.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute Zoom consultation or call/text me directly at (310) 850-1789. I'll learn about your business, show you examples of what I can build, and answer any questions. No pressure, no obligation.
